     1→Build a Full-Stack Amaravati Land Allotment & Development Management Portal
     2→1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
     3→
     4→Build a production-ready, enterprise-grade web application for the Amaravati Land Allotment & Development Approval Process under APCRDA.
     5→
     6→The portal must digitally manage the complete lifecycle of land allotment:
     7→
     8→Land Inventory → Investor Registration → Application → Eligibility → DPR → Technical Review → Economic Development Review → LASC → GoM → Cabinet Sub-Committee → APCRDA Authority → Cabinet Approval where applicable → Government Order → LOI → Payment → Revised DPR → Agreement → Registration → Possession → Building Permission → Construction Monitoring → Utilisation Compliance → Completion
     9→
    10→The application must support:
    11→
    12→Application submission
    13→Multi-level review
    14→Workflow routing
    15→Decision gates
    16→Document management
    17→GIS/land parcel information
    18→Financial/payment tracking
    19→Committee proceedings
    20→Government approvals
    21→Building permissions
    22→Construction progress monitoring
    23→Grievances and appeals
    24→Cancellation/resumption
    25→Notifications
    26→SLA monitoring
    27→Reports
    28→Dashboards
    29→Audit trails
    30→Role-based access
    31→Configurable workflow
    32→User and department management
    33→
    34→The application should look and feel like a modern government enterprise portal, with a clean, professional, secure and data-heavy interface.
    35→
    36→Do NOT build a simple CRUD application.
    37→
    38→Build a complete workflow-driven case management system.
