Build a Full-Stack Amaravati Land Allotment & Development Management Portal
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Build a production-ready, enterprise-grade web application for the Amaravati Land Allotment & Development Approval Process under APCRDA.

The portal must digitally manage the complete lifecycle of land allotment:

Land Inventory → Investor Registration → Application → Eligibility → DPR → Technical Review → Economic Development Review → LASC → GoM → Cabinet Sub-Committee → APCRDA Authority → Cabinet Approval where applicable → Government Order → LOI → Payment → Revised DPR → Agreement → Registration → Possession → Building Permission → Construction Monitoring → Utilisation Compliance → Completion

The application must support:

Application submission
Multi-level review
Workflow routing
Decision gates
Document management
GIS/land parcel information
Financial/payment tracking
Committee proceedings
Government approvals
Building permissions
Construction progress monitoring
Grievances and appeals
Cancellation/resumption
Notifications
SLA monitoring
Reports
Dashboards
Audit trails
Role-based access
Configurable workflow
User and department management

The application should look and feel like a modern government enterprise portal, with a clean, professional, secure and data-heavy interface.

Do NOT build a simple CRUD application.

Build a complete workflow-driven case management system.

One important addition

I would not let the AI coding agent treat the workflow as a simple linear sequence. Your source specifically identifies conditional decision gates—for example, Cabinet approval is only required when configured acreage/value/concessional/sensitive-land conditions are met—and parallel grievance and cancellation paths operate alongside the main workflow.

So the most important architectural components are:

Workflow Engine + Decision Engine + RBAC + Audit Trail + Document Management + GIS + SLA/Alert Engine.

That combination will make the portal scalable when APCRDA later changes approval rules, departments, thresholds, committee structures, or SLAs.

Also, the uploaded workflow itself notes that committee constitutions, thresholds and timelines should be validated against current APCRDA notifications/standing orders before production adoption
